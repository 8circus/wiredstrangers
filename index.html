About the band

Wired Strangers.

Writing songs about the things that keep you up at night.

Since 2015, four strangers making music the hard way: recording in Poznań in a mosquito-filled garden cottage, cycling 1000km to Montpellier on cargo bikes, playing shows on solar power, raising money for four charities along the way. Never quite the classical rock band script.

In May 2026, they walked into Number Nine Studios with producer Ian Clément and came out with an EP called Little Traveller. Stay tuned for the release.

They've opened for De Mens, played Les Estivales in Montpellier, and a handful of stages across Belgium, France and Poland.
